# IULinux book — v0.3.5

**Irfan Uruchi · 22 November 2026**

First public development edition of *IULinux: Designing and Building a Linux Distribution: Architecture, Implementation, and Testing*.

The 70-page PDF covers the IULinux Alpha image and installer, source-to-installed-system tracing, Linux boot and systemd, APT/dpkg and PackageKit transactions, KDE Settings and D-Bus, the ASUS G615LM power case, firmware, laboratory tests, and recovery. It includes 13 chapters, nine figures, twelve tables, and 32 bibliography entries.

**Scope:** IULinux-specific details are based on the 23 October 2026 Alpha project record and the 15 November 2026 ASUS investigation. The October source commit was not established for this edition. Proposed mechanisms and test fixtures are distinguished from observed behavior.

**Release assets:** PDF and its SHA-256 checksum. Editable manuscript sources are not distributed publicly.
