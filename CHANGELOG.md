# Changelog

## v0.3.5 — 22 November 2026

First prepared public book edition. Restores and expands technical coverage of EFI, initramfs diagnosis, image builds, package selection, desktop controls, OEM power profiles, firmware, and test procedures while retaining the improved typography and diagrams of the earlier manuscript. Contains 70 pages, 13 chapters, nine figures, twelve tables, and 32 cited bibliography entries.

Earlier manuscript editions v0.1–v0.3.4 were internal development revisions and are not included in this public repository.
